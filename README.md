This repository contains my Day 7 practical laboratory work for the Generative AI for Business course as a BBA Aviation Management student. The activity focuses on using Generative AI as a guided drafting assistant for professional aviation workplace communication.

The lab introduces a six-part structured email prompt consisting of Role, Task, Context, Constraints, Output Format, and Tone. It demonstrates how these components can be used to create clear, professional and context-specific aviation emails. The activity also introduces the BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) framework for urgent operational communication and the PAS (Problem, Agitation and Solution) framework for persuasive communication.

The project includes a weak flight-delay prompt, a structured prompt, generated email outputs, and a comparison of both approaches. A four-dimension review gate is applied to evaluate Accuracy, Tone, Compliance and Context. The lab also covers removing unsupported compensation, refund, meal, hotel and policy commitments before business use.

Additional activities include creating a passenger-complaint response, an internal airport staff email, adapting communication for passengers, senior management and operations staff, peer review, revision and reflection.

This work demonstrates practical AI-assisted workplace-writing skills for aviation management while emphasising factual accuracy, professional communication, compliance, audience awareness and human approval before important business communication is used.
